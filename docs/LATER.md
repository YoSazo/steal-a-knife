# Parked for later (decided 2026-10-07)

Ideas we agreed on but deliberately left until the prize-pick A/B test ends and the pen / coffin /
character rebuild ships. One experiment at a time; each gets its own A/B test.

## Next experiments, in order
1. **Round pace:** intermission 6 min -> ~4 min (rounds stay ~2.5). With coffins opening on the round
   clock, every round now pays the hub back. Judge on: stayed for a 2nd round, session length, D1.
   Don't go under ~4 min (deep biomes are up to 370 studs; heists can't feel squeezed).
2. **Pens + characters + coffins** (Studio prototypes built: PenPrototype, KnifeHaze, CoffinService
   round clock). Key metric: "left within 2 min of the first knife home" (24% on new builds).
3. **x2 Double Prize on the prize-pick card** (instead of the flickering pill on the timer line):
   "Double it! x2 for R$25" under the chosen prize. Measure purchases per round shown.
4. **Innocent power pick card** before the round (same principle as the prize pick): only ~65 of
   400 players ever used a power.

## Robux ideas around coffins / pens (not built)
- **Open now** is built (R$19, product CoffinOpen, Id 0 - create with analytics `npm run setup`).
  Later: price by rounds left (1 round cheap, 5 rounds more), like Steal an Egg's Skip Growth.
- **Open ALL my coffins now** - one bigger button when several are waiting.
- **Coffin Luck** (timed boost): better size / mutation rolls when your coffins open this hour.
- **Extra coffin slot** in the pen (if we cap how many coffins can wait at once).
- **Rarer coffin from the Robux case shop** (paid random policy applies - check PaidRandomRestricted).
- **Coffin Insurance**: a coffin stolen from your pen comes back.
- **Golden Shovel / Graverobber pass**: pick which coffin on a boss's graveyard you steal first, or
  see one coffin's contents before stealing.
- **Pen expansion** (bigger fence) as the Robux version of the cash upgrade.
- **Aura upgrades**: a stronger haze / rainbow layer on one knife (pure flex).
- **Rename your knife character** (status, self-expression).

## Symbiosis ideas (hub <-> rounds), parked
- Kidnap & rescue: the Murderer's loot sits caged in their pen with a sad face; rescue it next round.
- Your character is the weapon: victims see "Cosmic Carl got you!" with its face on the death card.
- Rounds pay in coffins: a coffin option on the prize card; Hero / Wipe coffins only from rounds.
- Server champion: most round wins = a giant statue of their best character in the plaza.
- Co-op carry: huge coffins carried faster by two players.
- Moon luck: coffins opening during the Thief Moon roll better mutations.
